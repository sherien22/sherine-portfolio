About Me

Passionate about building software that makes a difference.

I'm Sherine Zarif Farhan, a Full-Stack Developer and Computer Science student at Assiut University. I enjoy building modern, scalable web applications from the ground up — taking an idea from a blank page to a fully functional product.

I'm currently deepening my full-stack expertise through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), a nationally recognised program by the Egyptian Ministry of Communications that equips developers with industry-level skills through hands-on training and real-world projects.

I have practical experience through a Full-Stack .NET internship and multiple web development projects, giving me direct exposure to backend architecture, database design, and the complete development lifecycle.

Beyond web development, I have knowledge of Cybersecurity fundamentals, which shapes how I approach secure coding and system design.

My goal is to grow as a professional software engineer, contribute to strong development teams, and — eventually — build my own technology company.
